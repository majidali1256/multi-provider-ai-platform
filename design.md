## Brand & Style

The Dark Mode OLED design system for the Multi-Provider AI Platform uses a Bento Grid Showcase pattern. It focuses on scannable value props and high information density without clutter, ideal for developer tools and AI dashboards.

## Colors

Primary `#1E293B` and Background `#0F172A` ground the design in a deep, cinematic dark mode that is OLED-friendly. Accent green `#22C55E` highlights key status indicators and active states. Borders use `#475569` to define structure without overwhelming contrast.

## Typography

Inter handles all typography, providing a technical, clean, and highly readable aesthetic perfect for developer interfaces and dense data displays.
